Privacy

Last updated July 2026.

SimpleMeals is a small, non-commercial meal-planning app used by a handful of households. This page exists so anyone using it knows plainly what's collected and why — it's deliberately short rather than the usual wall of legal boilerplate.

What's collected

Signing in with Google shares your name, email, and profile photo with this app, the same information Google's own sign-in screen shows you before you approve it. Everything you enter afterward — recipes, meal plans, pantry items, requests — is stored so it can be shared with the rest of your household and shown back to you.

Who processes it

Data is stored with Supabase, the database and authentication provider this app runs on. Signing in goes through Google. Neither company is paid by this app to use your data for anything beyond making the app work, and nothing here is sold to anyone.

Analytics

With your consent — asked for the first time you visit, and changeable anytime via "Cookies" in the app — this app uses Google Analytics to see which pages get used. It doesn't run until you say yes. If you decline, no analytics cookie is set and no usage data is sent. Signing in still works either way; analytics has nothing to do with the app's actual functionality.

Who can see what

Anyone in your household — meaning anyone holding your household's invite code — can see and edit your recipes, meal plan, pantry, and grocery list. A recipe you mark "shared to the library" becomes visible to other households using this app; your meal plan, pantry, and grocery list never do, regardless of that setting.

Deleting your data

This is a small project without a self-serve deletion tool yet. If you'd like your account or household data removed, ask whoever administers your household's copy of this app.

Changes

If what this app collects or how it's used changes meaningfully, this page will be updated to reflect it.
